Delete your Moonhowl account

Moonhowl · com.moonhowl.app

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Moonhowl and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type your username to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you can’t open the app, email moonhowlofficial@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. Use the subject Delete my Moonhowl account and include your @username. We will acknowledge your request within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, profile photo, name, username, bio and links.
  • Your Moon-notes, comments, likes, bookmarks and follower connections.
  • Your notifications, block list and push notification device registration.

What we may keep

Any remaining personal information is deleted or anonymised within 30 days. We may keep a limited set of records for longer where the law requires or to resolve disputes, such as records of consent and reports about abuse. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.
