Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

1. About this Policy

Moonhowl is a community app where members, called Howlies, share writing, follow each other and take part in community activities. Moonhowl is operated by Kevin Antony ("Moonhowl", "we", "us"), based in India.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use, share and protect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”).

By creating an account and accepting this Policy, you agree to the handling of your information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use Moonhowl.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password or one-time passcodes (OTPs) used to verify and secure your account.
  • Profile details: your name or pen name, username, date of birth, profile photo, bio, and links to your other profiles or websites if you add them.
  • Content you create: your Moon-notes, comments, tags and mentions.
  • Reports and messages you send us, such as bug reports, verification requests and complaints.
  • Records of your consent, including which versions of our Terms and this Policy you accepted, and when.

Information created as you use Moonhowl

  • Your activity: who you follow and who follows you, what you like or bookmark, who you block, and the reports you make about other content or members.
  • Notifications we send you and whether they have been read.

Information collected automatically

  • A device token that lets us send you push notifications.
  • Basic technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language and IP address, and error or crash information that helps us keep Moonhowl stable and secure.

What we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, we do not collect your precise location, and we do not use advertising identifiers. Moonhowl only accesses your camera or photo gallery when you choose to add a profile photo.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account and verify that it is really you;
  • confirm that you are 18 or older;
  • provide Moonhowl’s features, including profiles, posting, comments, following, bookmarks and notifications;
  • send service messages such as verification codes, security alerts and important updates;
  • review reports, moderate content, enforce our Terms and keep the community safe;
  • find and fix bugs and improve performance;
  • meet our legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your writing to train third-party AI models.

4. Your consent

We process your personal data based on your consent, which you give when you accept this Policy at signup, and for certain legitimate purposes the law permits, such as keeping Moonhowl secure and complying with legal obligations.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account in Settings, or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide Moonhowl to you. It does not affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew.

5. What others can see

Your name, username, profile photo, bio, profile links, Moon-notes and comments can be seen by other Howlies. Please think before sharing personal details in public spaces.

Your email address and date of birth are never shown on your profile. The number of likes on your Moon-notes is visible only to you. If you block someone, you and that person stop seeing each other’s Moon-notes and comments.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • Service providers who help us run Moonhowl. These include Supabase (secure database, sign-in and file storage), Mailjet (sending verification emails) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications). They process information only to provide their service to us and are bound by confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Authorities, when required by law, a court order or a valid legal request.
  • To protect the rights, safety or property of Moonhowl, our members or the public, including investigating breaches of our Terms.
  • A change of ownership, if Moonhowl is ever transferred. We would tell you first, and the new owner would have to honour this Policy.
  • Anyone else, only with your consent.

7. Storage, transfers and security

Your information is stored with reputable cloud providers and may be processed on servers outside India. Where that happens we take reasonable steps to keep it protected.

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections, access controls and row-level security in our database. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a data breach affects your information, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide Moonhowl.

When you delete your account, your profile, photo, Moon-notes, comments, likes, bookmarks and follower connections are removed. We delete or anonymise your remaining personal information within 30 days. We may keep a limited set of records for longer where the law requires or to resolve disputes, such as records of consent and reports about abuse. Backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

If we ever stop being able to offer Moonhowl, we will delete personal data that we no longer need.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update your data (you can edit your profile at any time in the app);
  • ask us to erase your data and delete your account;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so;
  • have your grievance heard and resolved.

To use any of these rights, contact us as described in Section 14. We may need to confirm your identity first.

10. Children

Moonhowl is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at signup and do not allow anyone under 18 to register. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it along with its data. If you think a child has joined, please tell us.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The Moonhowl app does not use advertising cookies or trackers. It stores small pieces of data on your device, such as your sign-in session and a few settings, so the app works properly and keeps you signed in. You can clear this by logging out, clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

12. Links to other sites

Profiles can link to other websites and social media. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before sharing information with them.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as Moonhowl grows or the law changes. If we make a material change we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this Policy was last updated. Continuing to use Moonhowl after a change takes effect means you accept the updated Policy.

14. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your privacy, contact Kevin Antony at moonhowlofficial@gmail.com, or use Contact support in Help and write “Privacy” in the subject.

We will acknowledge your message within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

15. Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the courts of India, without affecting any rights you have under mandatory law.
