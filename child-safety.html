Child Safety Standards

Moonhowl

Moonhowl is a community app for writers. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behavior is prohibited on Moonhowl.

Age requirement

Signup includes a minimum-age check based on date of birth. People who do not meet the minimum age cannot register.

Reporting

Every note, comment and profile can be reported inside the app. Reports go to a moderation queue that is reviewed by the founder. Users can also block any account, which hides that person's content from them.

Enforcement

  • Violating content is deleted.
  • The responsible account is suspended.
  • Suspended email addresses cannot sign up again.

Legal compliance

Where required by law, we report CSAE content to the relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or local law enforcement.
